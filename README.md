# NOMAD RIDE CO. — Cinematic Motorcycle Rental Demo

A premium, mobile-responsive motorcycle rental website concept designed as a presentation demo for rental businesses. It uses full-screen riding video, editorial photography, custom motion graphics, a subtle telemetry HUD, scroll reveals, a moving film-strip ticker and a local-only enquiry preview.

## Live concept

https://shivansh-sec.github.io/rideline-cinematic-demo/

## Visual direction

- Dark cinematic film treatment with electric-lime and ember accents.
- Two muted looping motorcycle videos with accessible pause/play controls.
- Downloaded Pexels photography stored locally in `assets/`.
- Motion graphics, animated orbital rings, telemetry-style display, scan-line overlays, a scroll progress indicator, cursor light and restrained 3D card tilt.
- Responsive mobile navigation, cards and enquiry flow.
- Reduced-motion support and no contact details connected to a real business.

## Demo behaviour

This site is illustrative. Vehicle categories, brand identity, copy and visuals are sample content. The enquiry form validates its dates and creates a preview that can be copied locally; it does not send, store or transmit the user's details and it is not a reservation/payment system.

To adapt the concept for a real client, replace the NOMAD RIDE CO. identity, fleet images and descriptions, business policies, address, hours, contact channels and customer content with owner-approved details. Add a real booking or enquiry integration only when the business provides an authorized destination and required privacy disclosures.

## Media

The cinematic riding videos are sourced from Pexels. The motorcycle photographs are from Pexels and are stored in the local `assets/` directory to avoid third-party hotlinking. Verify current licensing and usage terms before any commercial launch.

## Local preview

The site is static: open `index.html` through a local development server or GitHub Pages.
